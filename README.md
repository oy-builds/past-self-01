# Two Lines to Your Past Self

A make.fun entry for "Two Lines to Your Past Self": a warning sent back to
one specific night — the 2 a.m. call from the hospital car park on the night
the writer turned sixteen — and the reason that missed call mattered: the
funeral was already over and the house already empty by the time he heard the
message.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the two lines to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`):

> When Dad calls from the hospital car park at two in the morning on the night you turn sixteen, pick up before the third ring.
> You let it ring out, and by the time you finally heard him say your mother was gone, the funeral was already over and the house was already empty.

Run it with `node main.ts`.
